# Healthcare Pharma Analytics

## Overview

Healthcare Pharma Analytics is an interactive Power BI dashboard project designed to analyze healthcare operations and pharmaceutical sales performance through data-driven visualizations and KPI reporting.

The dashboard combines healthcare analytics with pharmaceutical business insights to provide a unified view of patient trends, treatment costs, product performance, sales growth, and distributor analysis. The project demonstrates how analytics can support strategic decision-making in healthcare and pharmaceutical organizations.

---

# Business Problem

Healthcare institutions and pharmaceutical companies generate large volumes of operational and sales data. Without centralized analytics, identifying trends, monitoring treatment costs, analyzing sales performance, and evaluating business growth becomes difficult.

This project aims to transform raw healthcare and pharmaceutical datasets into actionable insights through interactive dashboards and visual analytics.

---

Objectives

* Analyze patient demographics and healthcare operational trends
* Monitor departmental treatment costs and diagnosis patterns
* Evaluate pharmaceutical sales performance and distributor contribution
* Track product-level revenue and yearly sales growth
* Support business decision-making using KPI-driven dashboards

---

Tools & Technologies

* Power BI
* Excel / CSV datasets
* Data Cleaning & Transformation
* KPI Reporting & Dashboard Visualization

---

# Dashboard Features

Healthcare Analytics

* Total patient analysis (10K+ patients)
* Monthly admission trend analysis
* Department-wise patient distribution
* Average treatment cost analysis
* Patient demographics by age and gender
* Most common diagnosis tracking
* Medicine discontinuation analysis

Pharmaceutical Sales Analysis

* Total pharmaceutical sales analysis
* Top-selling product identification
* Product class performance tracking
* Distributor-wise sales analysis
* Sales growth trend analysis
* Sub-channel sales distribution
* Top-performing city analysis

---

Key KPIs

* Total Patients: 10K
* Most Common Diagnosis: Migraine
* Most Expensive Medicine: Lisinopril
* Medicine Discontinued Rate: 33.58%
* Total Pharmaceutical Sales: $11.80 Billion
* Top Product: Ionclotide
* Top Product Class: Analgesics
* Top City by Sales: Butzbach

---

Key Insights

* Neurology recorded the highest average treatment cost among all departments.
* Migraine emerged as the most frequently diagnosed condition across patients.
* Pharmaceutical sales demonstrated strong growth trends across multiple years.
* Analgesics contributed significantly to overall product sales performance.
* Government and institutional channels accounted for a major share of total sales distribution.
* Certain pharmaceutical products generated exceptionally high revenue concentration compared to others.

---

Business Recommendations

* Increase focus on high-performing pharmaceutical product categories to maximize revenue growth.
* Improve inventory forecasting and supply planning for high-demand medicines.
* Monitor treatment cost variations across departments to improve operational efficiency.
* Strengthen distributor partnerships in high-performing sales regions.
* Use demographic and diagnosis trends to support strategic healthcare planning and targeted business decisions.
